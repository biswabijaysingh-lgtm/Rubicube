Info

  • Turn a layer — drag a sticker along the direction you want it to move.
  • Rotate the view — drag the empty background. Zoom with a pinch or the mouse wheel.
  • Scramble it mixes the cube; the timer starts on your first move.
  • Undo / Redo step back and forward through every move. Speed changes how fast the cube turns.
  • Solver shows the moves that solve the cube. Play them all, or step through one at a time.
  • Patterns builds a famous pattern on the 3×3. Moves turns the cube with buttons or typed notation like R U R' U'.
  • Notation — a letter is a clockwise quarter turn of that face (R, L, U, D, F, B), ' is counter-clockwise, 2 is a half turn. M, E, S are the middle slices.
  • Sound — clicks on every turn and a chime when you win. Use the speaker button to mute.
  • Keyboard — R L U D F B turn a face (Shift = counter-clockwise), Space scramble, Z undo, Y redo, arrows rotate the view.
